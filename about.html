About Me

My Story

I'm Yusuf Abdurahman, and I'm from Toronto. I've loved technology for most of my life, and I grew up in a city surrounded by people from every background, which is a big part of who I am today.

I moved to Oshawa to study Information Technology at Ontario Tech University. Leaving home for school was a big step, but it pushed me to become more independent and to build a new community from scratch.

I'm most interested in networking and cybersecurity. I like understanding how systems connect and how to keep them safe, and I learn best by building things and fixing them when they break.
